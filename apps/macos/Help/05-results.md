# Results and Style

This page shows you how to read a recipe's results and check it against a beer style.

## Read the results

The results strip shows the main numbers for the recipe. Click it to see every number in the **Results** tab.

| Result | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **OG** (original gravity) | The sugar in the wort before fermentation. A higher number means a stronger beer. |
| **FG** (final gravity) | The sugar left after fermentation. |
| **ABV** | The alcohol by volume. |
| **IBU** | The bitterness. About 20 is mild, about 40 is hoppy, and 60 or more is a strong IPA. |
| **SRM** | The color. 2 is pale straw, 10 is amber, and 30 or more is black. |

Hover over a result to see a short explanation of it.

## Check against a style

1. Open the **Style** tab.
2. Click **Choose a Style…**, type to search the BJCP styles, and pick one. Or click **New Recipe from a Style…** to start from one.
3. Read the style's figures under the choice: the BJCP guideline's range for each vital statistic. Then check where your recipe falls.

If a number falls outside the range, change the recipe until it fits, or keep it on purpose.

## Match the numbers to your system

The estimates depend on your brewhouse efficiency. After a brew day, enter the efficiency you measured under **Brewhouse efficiency** in the **Equipment** tab.
