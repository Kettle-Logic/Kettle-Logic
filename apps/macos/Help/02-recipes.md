# Recipes

This page shows you how to open, save, organize, delete, and share your recipes.

## Open a recipe

- Click a recipe in the sidebar.
- To open a recipe file, press **⌘O**, or click **Open…**. Kettle Logic opens BeerJSON (`.json`) files.

To make a copy of a recipe, right-click it in the sidebar, and then click **Duplicate Recipe**.

To rename a recipe, right-click it in the sidebar, and then click **Rename Recipe…**. You can also change the name in the **Name** field at the top of the recipe.

## Organize recipes into folders

1. Click the **New Folder** button in the sidebar.
2. Enter a name for the folder.
3. Drag a recipe onto the folder to move it there.

To move a recipe back to the top of the list, right-click it, and then choose **Top Level**.

To rename or delete a folder, right-click it, and then choose **Rename Folder…** or **Delete Folder**.

## Save your work

- To keep your changes saved as you go, leave **Autosave changes** on in **Settings**.
- To save by hand, press **⌘S**, or click **Save**.
- To save a copy under a new name, press **⇧⌘S**, or click **Save As…**.
- To undo a change, press **⌘Z**. To redo it, press **⇧⌘Z**. The Edit menu shows the name of the change.

## Delete a recipe

1. Right-click the recipe in the sidebar, and then click **Delete Recipe**.
2. Click **Delete Recipe** again in the confirmation.

The recipe moves to **Recently Deleted**, at the bottom of its document in the sidebar, and stays there until you bring it back or delete it for good. To bring it back, drag it out of that folder, or right-click it and click **Restore**. It returns to the top of the list. Press **⌘Z** right after you delete it to undo the move.

To delete a recipe for good, right-click it in **Recently Deleted**, click **Delete Permanently**, and then confirm. You can undo that until you quit the app.

To delete recipes without the confirmation, turn off **Confirm before deleting a recipe** in **Settings**. A recipe in **Recently Deleted** always asks first.

## Share a recipe

1. Click the **Share** button in the recipe's toolbar.
2. Under **Export As**, pick a format, and then choose where to save the file.
3. To send the file instead, pick a format under **Share As**.

Kettle Logic can export these formats:

- **BeerJSON**: a file that other brewing apps can open.
- **BeerXML**: a file for BeerSmith and other apps that use BeerXML.
- **Text**: a plain-text recipe sheet.
- **PDF**: the recipe as printable pages, with its estimates, ingredients, mash, and fermentation. To write values on during the brew, print a brew sheet instead (see Brew Day).

## Add a price

Enter a price on each ingredient, and the recipe shows what a batch costs. Kettle Logic uses the prices you enter. It does not look them up.
