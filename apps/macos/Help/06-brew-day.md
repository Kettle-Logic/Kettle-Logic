# Brew Day

This page shows you how to follow a brew-day procedure, use timers, and log a batch in the brew journal.

## Make a procedure

1. Open the recipe, and then click the **Procedure** tab.
2. Enter the boil length, in minutes.
3. Click **Generate Procedure**.
4. Click **Open Brew-Day View** to see the steps as a checklist.

To paste the steps somewhere else, click **Copy Procedure**.

## Check off steps

1. In the brew-day window, click a step to tick it off.
2. To untick every step, click **Start Over**.

Ticked steps stay ticked if you close the window.

## Use a timer

Click the **Start** button for a timed step, such as **Start 60-minute timer**. When the time is up, Kettle Logic shows **Time's up**.

- To pause the timer, click **Pause**.
- To continue, click **Resume**.
- To begin the timer again, click **Restart**.

## Fill in a brew sheet

A brew sheet is a printed page for the brew day, which you fill in with a pencil.

1. Open the recipe, and then click the **Brew Journal** tab.
2. Choose a session, and then click **Print Brew Sheet…** to save the sheet as a PDF. Print it.
3. On brew day, write each value in its box. Each box has a code, such as **B1** for the mash pH.
4. When the brew is done, click **Enter Brew Sheet…**, and type each value into the box with the same code. Leave empty boxes blank.
5. Click **Enter**. If a box holds text the sheet cannot read, or a value out of range, Kettle Logic lists it and enters nothing, so you can fix the box and try again.

The readings in section F replace the session's readings when the sheet has any. The sheet is in the units you chose in Settings.

## Log a batch in the brew journal

1. Open the recipe, and then click the **Brew Journal** tab.
2. Click **New Session**.
3. Under the session, click **Add Reading**. Enter the date, the temperature, and the gravity.
4. To save your readings as a spreadsheet, click **Export CSV…**. To load readings, click **Import CSV…**.

A recipe can have more than one session. To remove a session, click **Delete Session**.
