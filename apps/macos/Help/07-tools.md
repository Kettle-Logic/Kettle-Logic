# Tools and Unit Tools

This page shows you how to open the brewing calculators and the unit converters.

## Open a tool

1. Open the **Tools** menu for brewing calculators, or the **Unit Tools** menu for unit converters.
2. Click the tool you want.

Each tool opens in its own window, so you can keep several open at once.

## Open a calculator from a recipe

Some calculators open with the recipe's figures already filled in. Look for these buttons in a recipe:

- **Yeast Starter Calculator…**
- **Correct a Hydrometer Reading for Temperature…**
- **Convert a Refractometer Reading…**
- A hop's name and time, which opens the bitterness calculator for that hop.

## Brewing calculators

- **Water Profile**: adjusts your start water with salts.
- **Infusion** and **Decoction Volume**: plan the strike water and the decoction.
- **Mash Adjustment**: changes the mash temperature by adding water.
- **Mash pH Calculator**: estimates the mash pH.
- **Sparge Acid**: finds the acid for your sparge water.
- **Yeast Starter**: sizes a starter for your batch.
- **Hydrometer Temperature Adjustment** and **Refractometer to Gravity**: correct your gravity readings.
- **Gravity to Alcohol**: finds the alcohol by volume.
- **Hop Bitterness**: estimates the bitterness of a hop addition.
- **Hop Age**: estimates the alpha acid lost in older hops.
- **Mead Nutrient**, **Backsweetening**, and **Sulfiting**: plan additions for meads, ciders, and wines.
- **Boil-Off**: predicts your post-boil volume.
- **Dilution**: finds how much water to add.
- **Gravity Adjustment**, **Weight and Volume**, and **Carbonation**: adjust gravity, convert weights, and plan carbonation.

## Unit converters

Open **Unit Tools** to convert between units of temperature, gravity, pressure, weight, and volume. Enter a value, and the other units update.
