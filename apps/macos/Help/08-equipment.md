# Brewing Systems

This page shows you how to choose and set up the brewing system a recipe uses.

## Choose a system for a recipe

1. Open the recipe, and then click the **Equipment** tab.
2. Pick the system you brew with from the **Brewing system** list.
3. Check the batch size, the efficiency, and the boil-off, which update to match.

To apply a system to a recipe that is already open, click **Apply to Recipe**. The change includes the batch size, the brewhouse efficiency, and the boil-off. Press **⌘Z** to undo it.

## Make a new system

1. Click **Equipment** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New Brewing System**.
3. Enter your tun and kettle sizes and your boil-off rate, and then save it.

To change a system, click **Edit**. To keep a copy, click **Duplicate**, or **Save a Copy…**.

## Set the default system

1. Open **Settings**, or press **⌘,**.
2. Under **Default brewing system**, pick the system that new recipes start with.

## Measure your boil-off

Boil a known volume of water for an hour, and then measure what is left. Enter the amount that evaporated, as a percentage of the volume, as your boil-off rate.
