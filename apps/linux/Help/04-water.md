# Water

This page shows you how to set up the water for a recipe. Skip this page if you use bottled water and make no adjustments.

## Enter your start water

1. Open the recipe, and then click the **Water Builder** tab.
2. Enter the minerals in your source water. Your water report lists them.
3. Enter the share of reverse osmosis or distilled water you plan to mix in.

## Add salts

1. Click **Add Salt**.
2. Pick the salt, such as calcium sulfate or calcium chloride.
3. Enter the amount, in grams.

The **Minerals (mg/L)** section shows the result as you add salts.

## Save and load a water profile

A water profile holds your water values and salts, so you can reuse them.

1. Open the main menu, then **Tools** ▸ **Water Profile**.
2. Enter your start water and salts, and then click **Save Profile**.
3. To use a saved profile, pick it under **Saved Water Profiles**, and then click **Load Profile**.

Loading a profile replaces the water and salts in the recipe.

## Check the mash pH

1. Open the main menu, then **Tools** ▸ **Mash pH Calculator**.
2. Enter the grain and the mash water.
3. Click **Add Acid** to add acid, and then read the estimated pH.

Measure your mash with a pH meter to check the estimate.
