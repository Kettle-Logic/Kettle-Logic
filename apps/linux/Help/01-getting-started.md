# Getting Started

Kettle Logic helps you design a recipe, check its numbers, and keep notes on brew day. This page shows you how to make your first recipe.

## Choose how much to see

The first time you open Kettle Logic, it asks how much you want to see.

1. Click **Simple** to see only the basics. This is the best choice if you are new to brewing.
2. Click **Full** to see every tab, including water, mash, and yeast starter.
3. Choose how much anonymous data to share: **Off**, **Limited**, or **Enhanced**. **Limited** is selected at first, and docs/privacy.md in the project lists exactly what each one sends.
4. Click **Continue**.

To switch later, open the **main menu** (the button with three lines at the top right) and choose **Settings**, or press **Ctrl+,**, and change **Simple mode** under **Interface**.

## Make your first recipe

1. Click **New Recipe from a Style…** on the welcome screen, or press **Ctrl+Shift+N**.
2. Pick a beer style, and then click **Next**.
3. Check the batch size and the ingredients on the review step.
4. Click **Create Recipe**.

To start from an empty recipe, press **Ctrl+N**, or click **Blank Recipe** on the welcome screen.

## Learn the layout

- The **sidebar** on the left lists your recipes. Click a recipe to open it.
- The **tabs** across the top of a recipe hold its sections, such as **Fermentables**, **Hops**, and **Results**. Click a tab to see that section. Press **Ctrl+1** through **Ctrl+9** to jump to a tab.
- The **results** strip shows the main numbers for the recipe. Click it to see all of them.
